BartleBot Privacy Notice

Effective November 11, 2026

BartleBot™ is offered by Amy E. Tabor, an individual in Houston, Texas. This notice explains what information is collected through bartlebot.com and through requests to evaluate BartleBot, and how it is used.

The short version

  • The BartleBot software sends nothing to us. It runs in your own Claude account and on your own files.
  • We collect only what you send when you ask for a trial or write to us.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for anyone's marketing.

What we collect

When you request a trial or write to us. You give us your name, your firm or organization, the state or states where you practice, your practice area, your email address, and anything else you choose to include, such as a GitHub username or feedback about the software.

When you visit bartlebot.com. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted by GitHub, which logs each visitor's IP address for security purposes. We do not receive those logs. GitHub's privacy statement describes its practices.

When you use BartleBot. Nothing. The software is a set of instructions and templates that runs inside your own Claude account and connected apps. We do not receive your documents, your prompts, your case files, or any record of how you use the software. Anthropic and the other services you connect handle that data under your own agreements with them.

How we use it

  • To decide on your request and send the invitation that gives you access.
  • To keep a record of who holds a license and when it began.
  • To answer you when you write to us or send feedback.
  • To tell you about an important update to the software or the license. We do not send marketing email.

Who sees it

Amy E. Tabor is the only person who reviews trial requests. Your information is stored in her email account and in her own files. The service providers that carry or store it (her email provider, her file storage provider, and GitHub, which sends the invitation to the email address or username you give us) process it only to provide those services. We do not otherwise disclose it unless the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you hold a BartleBot license, and afterward as a record of the license, until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

Email the address below to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. If you ask us to delete it, we will do so and will keep only what the law requires us to keep.

Children

BartleBot is for lawyers and legal professionals. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
